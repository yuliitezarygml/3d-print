# Code of Conduct

[Русский](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) · [English](CODE_OF_CONDUCT_EN.md)

We want participation in PrintForge to be safe and professional for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Respect contributors and their experience level.
- Discuss code and ideas, not personalities.
- Give specific, verifiable, and constructive feedback.
- Respect customer privacy and copyright in 3D models.
- Acknowledge mistakes and help correct them.

## Unacceptable behavior

Insults, discrimination, threats, harassment, publication of another person's private data or secrets, spam, and deliberate disruption of the project are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report violations to the repository owner through a private GitHub channel. The owner may hide or remove content, close discussions, and restrict participation. Reports are handled confidentially to the extent possible.
